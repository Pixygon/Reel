# Test fixtures

- `fixture.mp4` — generated with ffmpeg (`testsrc`), 320×240, ~2 s.
- `speech.wav` — the "ask not what your country can do for you" excerpt from
  President Kennedy's 1961 inaugural address, as distributed in the
  [whisper.cpp](https://github.com/ggml-org/whisper.cpp) repository
  (`samples/jfk.wav`). The recording is a US Government work and is in the
  public domain. It is used here so the caption test can assert that real
  words come back from a real transcription.
